# SIH Judge Demonstration Guide

## 3–4 minute flow
1. Command Center — establish the fragmented-material problem.
2. Material Catalog — open a CPSE material record.
3. AI Standardization — show standardized attributes/identity.
4. Error Detection — open a duplicate/non-standard/anomaly case.
5. Match reasoning — show candidate evidence and technical fields.
6. False-Merge Firewall — introduce a critical mismatch such as grade, dimension, pressure class or voltage.
7. LIORA — ask why the candidate was blocked and request the evidence.
8. Governance — show human review and audit trail.
9. Cross-CPSE insights — show commonality, inventory or procurement context.
10. Close — show the live URL/QR.

## Suggested LIORA questions
- Explain SIH26099.
- What is Engineering DNA?
- Why is semantic similarity not enough?
- Why was this material rejected?
- Show the evidence behind this decision.
- Explain this legacy mapping.
- What changes if the grade changes?
